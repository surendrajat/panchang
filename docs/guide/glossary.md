# Glossary — the Sanskrit / Hindi terms

Every term the course uses, with Devanagari, a transliteration, the **literal**
meaning (which is often the best mnemonic), and the lesson it appears in. Grouped
by theme so related words sit together; use your browser's find (Ctrl-F) for a
quick lookup.

A note on transliteration: long vowels are marked (ā, ī, ū), and ṛ, ṣ, ś, ṇ, ṭ,
ḍ, ṁ are the standard IAST diacritics. Don't worry about pronouncing them
perfectly — they're here so the words are unambiguous.

## Tropical, sidereal, precession (Lesson 4)

| Term | Devanagari | Literally | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sāyana | सायन | "with ayana" | **tropical** — longitude from the (moving) equinox |
| nirayana | निरयन | "without ayana" | **sidereal** — longitude from the fixed stars |

## Months & the year (Lessons 9–10)

| Term | Devanagari | Literally | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| māsa | मास | "month/Moon" | a lunar month · *[9](./09-masa.md)* |
| amānta | अमान्त | "new-moon-ending" | month = new moon → new moon (South) |
| pūrṇimānta | पूर्णिमान्त | "full-moon-ending" | month = full moon → full moon (North) |

| kṣaya | क्षय | "to shrink, diminish" | of a tithi: too short, so dropped · *[6](./06-tithi.md)* |
| vṛddhi | वृद्धि | "to grow, increase" | of a tithi: too long, so counted twice · *[6](./06-tithi.md)* |
| nija | निज | "one's own, true" | the *real* month, vs an adhika (leap) one |
| saṅkrānti | सङ्क्रान्ति | "going across" | the Sun entering a new rāśi · *[10](./10-the-year.md)* |
| ṛtu | ऋतु | "season" | one of the **six** seasons · *[10](./10-the-year.md)* |
| saṁvat | संवत् | "year" | an era year-count (Vikrama, Śaka, …) · *[10](./10-the-year.md)* |
